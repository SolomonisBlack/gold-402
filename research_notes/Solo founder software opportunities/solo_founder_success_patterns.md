# Solo Founder Software Success Patterns (2025–2026): Outcomes, Economics, Distribution, Failure Modes

Research date: November 6, 2026. Source quality: most revenue figures for named founders are self-reported, either on X or on revenue dashboards, and reach us through secondary blogs. TrustMRR is the exception because it pulls figures from payment providers through read-only keys. Some numbers below are labeled "secondary" or "unverified". The report writer should keep those labels.

## 1. Documented solo-founder software successes (2024–2026): what was built, revenue, why it worked

### Takeaway
A small set of famous solo founders earn $1M or more a year: Levels, Marc Lou, Tony Dinh, Postma, and Base44's Maor Shlomo. Almost all of them already had a large audience or a strong paid-acquisition engine before the product launched. Stripe-verified data from TrustMRR shows the typical outcome is far more modest: about two-thirds of listed startups are under $1K, and only about 15% exceed $10K. Revenue correlates with startup age and domain authority (SEO) more strongly than with X follower count.

### Cited Findings
**Aggregate verified data (TrustMRR, Marc Lou's Stripe-verified revenue database)**
- TrustMRR reads revenue from payment providers through read-only API keys and ranks startups on a public leaderboard. It describes itself as "the database of verified startup revenues", is run by Marc Lou, and takes no VC money. — [1capture TrustMRR review](https://www.1capture.io/blog/trustmrr-review)
- Total verified revenue on TrustMRR is $1.96B across 69M transactions. Distribution of startups by revenue: $0–$1K 67.7%, $1K–$10K 16.9%, $10K–$100K 10.3%, $100K–$1M 4.0%, $1M+ 1.0%. The page does not say whether these buckets are MRR or all-time revenue. — [TrustMRR stats](https://trustmrr.com/stats)
- Founder X follower distribution on TrustMRR: 53.4% have 0–1K followers, 12.1% have 1K–10K, 2.4% have 10K–100K, and 0.3% have 100K+. — [TrustMRR stats](https://trustmrr.com/stats)
- TrustMRR correlations: revenue vs. founder X followers r=0.25 (n=6,223); revenue vs. startup age r=0.46 (n=7,735); revenue vs. Domain Rating (SEO authority) r=0.43 (n=6,964). — [TrustMRR stats](https://trustmrr.com/stats)
- TrustMRR lists high-revenue startups whose founders have fewer than 100 X followers. Examples: "Shopquantum" at $2.12M (80 followers), "Fast Scaling SaaS" at $1.28M (40 followers), and "Marky" at $1.02M (67 followers). — [TrustMRR stats](https://trustmrr.com/stats)
- Top TrustMRR categories by total revenue, in order: E-commerce, Content Creation, Sales, Entertainment, Analytics, Education, SaaS, Social Media, Customer Support, Mobile Apps. Content Creation shows 256% 30-day growth and Sales shows 154%. — [TrustMRR stats](https://trustmrr.com/stats)
- Median all-time revenue by payment provider: Superwall $3,649; Shopify $1,015; RevenueCat $375 (15.6% of startups); Stripe $279 (54.7%); LemonSqueezy $122; Paddle $42; Polar $14 (7.9%); DodoPayments $3 (12.5%). — [TrustMRR stats](https://trustmrr.com/stats)
- Example of a solo-founder listing: Alchie, a B2C social media product, at about $11.6K MRR in October 2026 according to a third-party write-up that cross-checks TrustMRR. — [Aiolum](https://aiolum.com/story/alchie/)

A later DEV post's headline says it "went to zero". That claim is unconfirmed. — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/promptway/he-built-a-flight-simulator-in-three-hours-and-hit-1m-a-year-in-17-days-then-it-went-to-zero-1b1l)
- **Levels' portfolio:** about $3M/yr across Photo AI, RemoteOK and others (secondary; date unclear). — [aiidelist](https://aiidelist.com/indie-developers/pieter-levels); [Ascero AI](https://asceroai.com/news/solo-founder-1m-arr-ai-coded-2026)

- **Danny Postma / HeadshotPro:** $1M ARR in under a year, later sold. He previously exited Headlime. No primary revenue figure was found. — [Ascero AI](https://asceroai.com/news/solo-founder-1m-arr-ai-coded-2026); [indieai](https://indieai.pages.dev/blog/top-5-successful-indie-developers-2026)

- **Ben Broca / Polsia:** said to have reached $1M ARR in 30 days. Only one source was found. — [Ascero AI](https://asceroai.com/news/solo-founder-1m-arr-ai-coded-2026)

**Exit and valuation context (Acquire.com)**

- The median was 3.9x profit (SDE) in both 2024 and 2025 for deals under $10M EV. Across 651 Acquire listings, the median asking price was $195,800, at about 2x revenue (asking, not closed). — [BigIdeasDB](https://bigideasdb.com/state-of-saas-valuations-2026)
- Acquire's 2025 report: multiples rose, and more deals used holdbacks (14%) and seller financing (13%). — [Paddle summary of Acquire 2025 report](https://paddle.com/news/industry/acquire-market-report-2025-saas-m-and-a)
- Unofficial summary: 3.7x for deals under $100K net income and 3.9x for $100K–$1M. — [video-summaries (unofficial)](https://video-summaries.pages.dev/acquisition-multiples-report-2025-findings.html)

### Inferences
- The famous names succeed through distribution they already had: Levels' and Marc Lou's audiences, and TypingMind riding ChatGPT demand while selling to Tony Dinh's existing developer following. They show what the ceiling looks like, not what a typical founder can expect.
- TrustMRR shows that a very large audience is not required. Many high-revenue entries have founders with fewer than 100 followers. The strongest predictors in its data are age and Domain Rating (SEO), which favors patient, search- and marketplace-driven businesses over launch spikes.
- Many recent entrants use DodoPayments and Polar, and their median revenue is close to zero ($3 and $14). This fits a wave of new, very early, vibe-coded products. Older providers like Stripe and Paddle show higher medians because their users have been around longer.
- At about 4x profit, a $5K MRR solo SaaS with roughly 80% margin is worth about $190K. The usual exit for a typical solo product is modest.

### Gaps
- No primary 2025 or 2026 data was found on time-to-first-revenue across solo founders. The examples here are anecdotes such as fly.pieter.com in 17 days and TypingMind at $10K/mo within months.
- Starter Story's revenue database was not accessed, because the searches surfaced only SEO blogs.
- Current audited figures for Postma, Dinh and fly.pieter.com were not found.
- TrustMRR's stats page does not give the total startup count or the solo share, and its timestamps are inconsistent.

## 2. The vibe-coding era: speed gains and their consequences (flooding, commoditization, "distribution is the moat")

### Takeaway
AI coding tools made building close to free. Lovable is reportedly at roughly $400–600M ARR with about 8M users, and Apple App Store submissions rose 84% YoY in Q1 2026. Downloads, meanwhile, grew only 2–3%, and RevenueCat data shows winners pulling away from everyone else. Supply exploded while demand stayed flat, so distribution, not code, is now the binding constraint.

### Cited Findings
- About 235,800 new App Store submissions in Q1 2026, up 84% YoY over Q1 2025 (Sensor Tower, as reported by the NYT and The Information). — [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/vibe-coding-apple-app-store-surge-crackdown); [WinBuzzer](https://winbuzzer.com/2026/04/07/vibe-coding-app-store-surge-apple-crackdown-xcxwbn/); [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/112818/vibe-coding-is-flooding-the-app-store-with-new-apps-on-track-for-record-submissions-in-2026/index.html)
- About 560,000 new apps in H1 2026. The App Store is on pace for more than 1M new apps in 2026, beating the 2016 record of 890K. Most submitters have little or no coding background. App downloads rose only about 3% last year and about 2% so far in 2026. — [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/113213-apple-app-store-inundated-low-quality-vibecoded-apps.html)
- Copycat apps, privacy-questionable clones, and apps that don't work as advertised are increasing. Former App Store chief Phillip Shoemaker warned that review resources may be strained. — [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/113213-apple-app-store-inundated-low-quality-vibecoded-apps.html); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/vibe-coding-apple-app-store-surge-crackdown)
- Apple blocked updates for Replit and Vibecode and removed the "Anything" app, arguing that in-app code generation breaks the rules against apps changing behavior after review. — [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/vibe-coding-apple-app-store-surge-crackdown); [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/apple-cracks-down-on-vibecoding-apps-as-app-store-submissions-surge)
- Outlets disagree on earlier growth rates. Some cite a 60% surge, or 56% monthly growth by December 2025. — [findskill.ai](https://findskill.ai/blog/vibe-coding-by-the-numbers/)
- RevenueCat 2026 (115,000+ apps, $16B+ revenue): more than 14,000 new apps enter the stores each month. The top 25% of apps grew MRR by 80%+ YoY while the bottom 25% shrank by more than 33%. The top 5% earned about 400x the bottom 25%, up from 200x in 2024. — [RevenueCat 2026 benchmarks](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md)
- **Lovable:** about $400M ARR with 146 employees (around February–March 2026). Later reports put it at about $500M (June 2026) and on track for $600M, with about 8M users and 60M+ projects. Figures come from press and aggregators and are not audited. — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/andrew-ooo/lovable-hits-400m-arr-with-146-employees-274-million-per-person-1i63); [DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/lovable-statistics/); [Ecosistema Startup](https://ecosistemastartup.com/lovable-cruza-us600m-arr-y-consolida-el-vibe-coding/)
- **Replit:** about $106M ARR in June 2025 (Sacra). Later figures conflict ($150M or $253M by October 2025). — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/replit-at-106m-arr)
- **Bolt.new:** about $40M ARR in early 2025 and 5M registered users by May 2025. No reliable 2026 figure was found. — [Luminix landscape report](https://www.useluminix.com/reports/industry-analysis/vibe-coding-tool-landscape-replit-v0-base44-bolt-lovable-vercel/source/3)
- **Product Hunt:** about 20 launches per day on average (Databox study of 4,962 launches; Tuesdays about 30, Saturdays about 11). AI is the largest category, with 1,111 AI launches in Q2 2026, up 46% YoY. Generic "AI for productivity" pitches finish below the top 20. — [mean.ceo PH stats](https://blog.mean.ceo/?p=11218); [LaunchList guide](https://f.getlaunchlist.com/blog/how-to-launch-on-product-hunt-2026)
- Founder commentary says Product Hunt is "a hub of builders and high-karma hunters, your end users aren't there". Launch traffic spikes and fades within about a week, and search and AI referrals form the lasting baseline. — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/product-hunt-is-a-hub-of-builders-and-high-karma-hunters-your-end-users-arent-there-6d18cffdae). Founders report spending 100–200 hours on a launch. — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/indiehackerksa/why-product-hunt-no-longer-works-for-indie-founders-aom)
- Commentary says AI coding tools removed the cost of writing code as the binding constraint on solo SaaS. — [agentmarketcap](https://agentmarketcap.ai/blog/2026/04/09/solo-founder-ai-agent-stack-1m-arr); [stormy.ai on $1M ARR via Google Ads + Claude Code](https://stormy.ai/blog/how-to-build-1m-arr-ai-app-2026)

### Inferences
- Any product that can be described in one sentence and rebuilt in a weekend with Lovable or Claude Code will be cloned. Its price ceiling falls toward zero, and a launch spike won't protect it.
- Durable advantages now come from things code generation cannot copy quickly: rankings that build up over time (SEO and Domain Rating, marketplace reviews and install counts), integrations and workflow lock-in, proprietary or domain data, trust and compliance, and owned audiences.
- Apple's crackdown and the review backlog add platform risk for iOS-first consumer apps. Web, B2B, and marketplace channels with steadier review processes look safer for a solo founder.

### Gaps
- No official Product Hunt per-day launch counts for 2025 versus 2023 were found.
- No data on Chrome Web Store or Shopify App Store submission growth caused by vibe coding was found. The only figure is Shopify's app count, covered in section 6.
- No reliable 2026 user or revenue figures for Bolt or Replit were found.

## 3. Product types being crushed by AI commoditization or foundation-model features (to avoid)

### Takeaway
Thin wrappers are the most exposed, especially single-prompt products: generic writing, chat-with-PDF, file analysis, and "custom GPT"-style assistants. Foundation-model companies keep absorbing these features for free. The evidence is mostly commentary, and few deaths are well documented. Jasper is often cited but did not die overnight. AI monthly subscriptions also retain worse than non-AI subscriptions.

### Cited Findings
- When ChatGPT added native PDF upload and analysis, outlets said it might knock out PDF-chat startups. Even at the time, it was unclear whether any startup actually died. — [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/beware-ai-startups-openai-chatgpt-can-take-you-out-1850985136); [TechRound](https://techround.co.uk/news/a-chatgpt-update-may-have-just-knocked-out-a-bunch-of-ai-startups/) (2023, older)
- Categories OpenAI absorbed within about two years: web browsing, file analysis, and custom GPTs. — [DEV "The Wrapper"](https://dev.to/thesythesis/the-wrapper-kc7)
- Custom GPTs took over a large share of wrapper functionality for free, and by 2025 users had stopped paying for the "AI" label alone. — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/jamilxt/ai-wrappers-are-dying-three-business-models-survived-instead-5db4)
- Jasper's leadership feared OpenAI had wiped them out overnight, but that did not happen. Thin wrappers with real users were not clearly doomed by GPT Store-style features. — [The Algorithmic Bridge](https://www.thealgorithmicbridge.com/p/openai-gpt-store-is-not-the-end-of) (older, 2023–24)
- About 3,800 AI startups reportedly shut down between 2023 and early 2025, a 27% failure rate (no source given, unverified). — [tianpan.co](https://tianpan.co/blog/tags/platform-risk)
- A blog claims a "~3% two-year survival rate" for wrappers, attributed to McKinsey. This could not be verified. — [alexcloudstar](https://www.alexcloudstar.com/blog/ai-wrappers-dead-what-to-build-instead-2026/)
- RevenueCat 2026: AI apps earn higher Year-1 realized LTV ($30.16 median vs. $21.37 for non-AI, a 41% premium), but AI monthly plans retain 36% worse over 12 months than traditional apps. — [RevenueCat 2026 benchmarks](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md)
- AI margin pressure: ICONIQ's 2026 State of AI reports average AI product gross margin of about 45% in 2025, projected to reach 53% in 2026. Inference costs run about 23% of revenue at scaling-stage AI B2B companies (secondary summaries; dates inconsistent). — [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/have-ai-gross-margins-really-turned-the-corner/); [softwareseni](https://www.softwareseni.com/why-ai-gross-margins-are-so-much-lower-than-saas-and-what-that-means-for-your-business/)
- Bessemer 2025: fast-ramping AI "Supernovas" average about 25% gross margin, and "Shooting Stars" about 60%. That compares with roughly 80–90% for classic SaaS (secondary). — [softwareseni](https://www.softwareseni.com/why-ai-gross-margins-are-so-much-lower-than-saas-and-what-that-means-for-your-business/); [Rework](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/ai-startup-growth/ai-vs-saas-gross-margins)
- Public SaaS companies reportedly show gross margins 10–17 points below their pre-AI baselines after adding AI features (single secondary source). — [softwareseni](https://www.softwareseni.com/what-the-ai-inference-cost-crisis-means-for-growing-software-companies/)

### Inferences
Categories to avoid or treat with extreme caution:
- Generic writing, rewriting and "AI copywriter" tools
- Chat-with-PDF or document Q&A
- Generic meeting summarizers
- Generic AI image or avatar generators. Photo AI and HeadshotPro won early, then the space flooded.
- ChatGPT UI shells. TypingMind won early and is distribution-dependent.
- Generic "AI agent for X" horizontal productivity apps
- Prompt marketplaces
- Any feature that sits on the roadmap of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft Copilot, Notion AI or Canva

Other inferences:
- Where AI belongs: an AI feature inside a workflow that owns data, integrations or compliance can survive. A product that is only the AI feature cannot. Usage-based or credit pricing protects margin. Flat $9/mo with unlimited LLM calls is a margin trap.
- Classic non-AI "boring" SaaS keeps 80–90% gross margins, which is a structural advantage for a solo founder who can't afford margin compression.

### Gaps
- No rigorous, named list of 2025–2026 solo AI wrapper shutdowns with revenue trajectories was found.
- Jasper's 2025 revenue and layoffs were not verified in this pass.
- SimpleClosure and Carta shutdown data for AI startups was not retrieved.

## 4. Characteristics that predict solo success now (niche vs. horizontal, B2B vs. B2C, pricing model, SEO, marketplaces)

### Takeaway
The best-supported predictors are:
- **Time and SEO authority:** TrustMRR shows r=0.46 for startup age and r=0.43 for Domain Rating, compared with r=0.25 for X followers.
- **A channel that already contains buyers:** marketplaces, search, or an existing audience. Launch platforms don't count.
- **Payment friction set to filter for real buyers:** hard paywalls and annual plans.
- **Low-competition niches with clear ROI and integration lock-in.**

Most "boring niche" evidence is blog-tier, but it points the same way as the verified data.

### Cited Findings
- Revenue correlates more strongly with Domain Rating (r=0.43) and startup age (r=0.46) than with founder X followers (r=0.25). — [TrustMRR stats](https://trustmrr.com/stats)
- E-commerce is the top TrustMRR category by total revenue. Startups paid through Shopify billing have the second-highest median all-time revenue ($1,015) of any payment provider. — [TrustMRR stats](https://trustmrr.com/stats)
- "Boring" B2B tools have clear buyers with budgets, pain that recurs monthly, and very low churn once embedded. Their profile is clear ROI, few niche-focused competitors, and a straightforward integration surface (blog-tier). — [saasblog](https://saasblog.pages.dev/posts/2026/01/boring-saas-niches-that-print-money-for-devs); [flowjam](https://flowjam.com/blog/indie-hackers-saas-ideas-2025-10-you-can-launch-fast)
- In r/SaaS discussions, founders report that a "boring" service-adjacent product, such as bookkeeping for contractors, was more profitable and sustainable than AI-first ideas (aggregated Reddit, anecdotal). — [discury](https://discury.io/playbook-profitable-boring-businesses-vs-ai-startups-8643)
- **Hard paywall vs. freemium (RevenueCat 2026):**
  - Day-35 trial-to-paid: 10.7% for hard paywalls vs. 2.1% for freemium.
  - Day-60 revenue per install: $3.09 vs. $0.38, about 8x.
  - One-year retention of yearly subscribers is about the same: 27% vs. 28%.
  — [RevenueCat 2026 benchmarks](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md)
- Trial length matters: 17–32 day trials convert at a 42.5% median, against 25.5% for trials under 4 days. — [RevenueCat 2026 benchmarks](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md)
- 2025 RevenueCat: higher-priced apps have a median download-to-trial of 9.8% vs. 4.3% for low-priced apps. — [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025)
- ChartMogul data (cited secondhand): below $25 ARPA, annual plans retain 62% vs. 41% for monthly. ChartMogul's December 2025 retention report found median NRR of 82% for B2B SaaS and 49% for B2C SaaS. — [search summary of ChartMogul data via Subjolt/Livmo](https://www.subjolt.com/guides/churn-rate-benchmarks/); [Livmo](https://livmo.com/blog/saas-churn-benchmarks-valuation/)
- Shopify App Store discovery depends on reviews, review velocity and the "Built for Shopify" badge. One ecosystem source says the store "no longer creates demand. It only converts it." The middle of a big category is called the worst position: platform-level costs at niche-utility revenue. — [ecomappguide](https://ecomappguide.pages.dev/posts/2026/02/shopify-apps-business-guide-for-builders); [BigIdeasDB](https://bigideasdb.com/how-to-sell-a-shopify-app)
- Case writeup: scaling a Shopify app to $4M ARR through App Store SEO (vendor blog). — [stormy.ai](https://stormy.ai/blog/shopify-app-store-seo-scale-4m-arr)
- Product Hunt is not a lasting channel; search and AI referrals form the lasting baseline. — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/product-hunt-is-a-hub-of-builders-and-high-karma-hunters-your-end-users-arent-there-6d18cffdae)

### Inferences
- **B2B over B2C:** B2B NRR is about 82% vs. 49% for B2C, and businesses pay higher ARPU and churn less. B2C works mainly with a hard paywall, annual plans, and an existing traffic engine. Photo AI is the example of the last point.
- **Niche over horizontal:** horizontal tools are where foundation models and vibe-coded clones compete. Vertical tools with domain-specific workflows, data formats, or compliance needs are harder to copy and easier to reach through targeted SEO.
- **Recurring and one-time both work if matched to the buyer:**
  - Recurring suits B2B with ongoing value and supports exit multiples.
  - One-time or lifetime licenses suit developer and prosumer tools (TypingMind; Marc Lou's boilerplates).
  - One-time licenses perform poorly when costs are per-use, as with LLM calls.
- **SEO, programmatic SEO, and "alternative to X" pages:** TrustMRR's Domain Rating correlation is the strongest verified evidence that compounding search assets predict revenue. No primary data specific to "alternative to X" pages was found.

### Gaps
- No primary data on conversion rates of programmatic SEO or "alternative to X" pages was found.
- No verified distribution of revenue for Slack, HubSpot, Notion or Atlassian Marketplace apps was retrieved.
- Starter Story's aggregate statistics were not accessed.

## 5. Economics: churn, ARPU, conversion, payment providers and merchant of record, LLM cost erosion

### Takeaway
Low-ARPU indie products churn about 6–9% a month (Baremetrics). B2B SaaS medians are far lower, around 3.5% (Recurly). The median subscription app earns under $50/month at its first anniversary. Merchant-of-record fees cluster around 4–6% effective. LLM features can cut gross margin from 80–90% to 25–60%.

### Cited Findings
- **Churn (Baremetrics Open Benchmarks, 800+ startups):**
  - Monthly user churn: about 5.8% at $10–$25 ARPU, up to about 8.4% at $50–$100 ARPU.
  - Monthly revenue churn: about 5.6% at $250+ ARPU, up to about 8.8% at $50–$100.
  - A second page on the same site lists different numbers, so verify before relying on these.
  — [Baremetrics](https://baremetrics.com/blog/saas-financial-benchmarks-by-baremetrics)
- **Churn (Recurly 2025):** average B2B SaaS churn is 3.5% (2.6 points voluntary, 0.8 points involuntary). — [Vitally](https://www.vitally.io/post/saas-churn-benchmarks)
- **Churn (Lighter Capital 2025):** median revenue churn at private startups rose from 11.34% to 12.50% (annualized). — [Lighter Capital](https://www.lightercapital.com/blog/2025-b2b-saas-startup-benchmarks)
- **Retention by plan and market (ChartMogul):** median NRR is 82% for B2B SaaS and 49% for B2C (December 2025). Below $25 ARPA, annual plans retain 62% vs. 41% for monthly (secondhand). — [Subjolt](https://www.subjolt.com/guides/churn-rate-benchmarks/); [Livmo](https://livmo.com/blog/saas-churn-benchmarks-valuation/)
- **Typical app outcomes (RevenueCat):**
  - The median subscription app makes under $50/month by its first anniversary (2025). — [SaaStr summary](https://saastr.com/the-top-10-learnings-from-revenuecats-state-of-subscription-apps-how-115000-mobile-apps-deliver-16b-in-revenue-whats-working-whats-quietly-killing-growth); [TechRepublic: "Most apps fail to hit $1,000 monthly revenue within two years"](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-apps-revenue-developers/)
  - Annual subscribers cancelling within year one rose from about 56% in 2025 to about 72% in 2026, and 35% of annual cancellations happen in Month 1. — [RevenueCat 2026 benchmarks](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md)
  - On 3-day trials, 55.4% of cancellations happen on Day 0 and 84% by Day 1. — [RevenueCat 2026 benchmarks](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md)
  - Involuntary billing failures cause 31% of Google Play cancellations (up from 28.2%) and 14% on the App Store. — [RevenueCat 2026 benchmarks](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md)
- **Merchant-of-record landscape and fees:**
  - Stripe acquired Lemon Squeezy in 2024 (reported close dates vary). Stripe's own MoR product, Stripe Managed Payments, entered public preview in February 2026, and some reports say the Lemon Squeezy team built it. Lemon Squeezy's long-term future is uncertain. — [PYMNTS](https://pymnts.com/?p=2018528); [devtoollab](https://devtoollab.com/blog/best-merchant-of-record-platforms); [alexcloudstar](https://alexcloudstar.com/blog/merchant-of-record-indie-saas-2026/)
  - Lemon Squeezy and Paddle: about 5% + $0.50 per sale. Paddle is still independent. — [dev.to fee comparison](https://dev.to/jettfu/stripe-vs-paddle-vs-lemon-squeezy-fee-comparison-2026-2c77); [subger](https://subger.com/en/service/lemonsqueezy)
  - Polar: 4% + $0.40, plus surcharges for international cards and subscriptions. It reportedly raised its headline rate by 25% in 2026. — [DEV devtoolpicks](https://dev.to/devtoolpicks/polar-vs-lemon-squeezy-vs-creem-in-2026-which-mor-is-right-for-indie-hackers-506m); [alexcloudstar](https://alexcloudstar.com/blog/merchant-of-record-indie-saas-2026/)
  - Stripe Managed Payments: 2.9% + $0.30 plus a 3.5% MoR fee, about 6% effective on a $50 sale. — [devtoollab](https://devtoollab.com/blog/best-merchant-of-record-platforms)
  - Creem and DodoPayments are newer MoRs aimed at indie hackers. On TrustMRR, DodoPayments has a 12.5% share of startups but a $3 median revenue. — [Creem blog](https://www.creem.io/blog/lemonsqueezy-alternatives-after-stripe-acquisition); [TrustMRR stats](https://trustmrr.com/stats)
- **Shopify App Store fees:** 0% revenue share on the first $1M. Sources conflict on whether this is a lifetime or annual allowance (from January 1, 2025), so check Shopify's partner docs. — [Shopify Help (DE)](https://help.shopify.com/de/partners/making-apps); [BigIdeasDB](https://bigideasdb.com/how-to-sell-a-shopify-app)
- **LLM margin erosion:** ICONIQ puts AI product gross margin at about 45% in 2025 and about 53% in 2026, with inference costs about 23% of revenue. Bessemer gives 25–60% for AI companies vs. 80–90% for SaaS. — [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/have-ai-gross-margins-really-turned-the-corner/); [softwareseni](https://www.softwareseni.com/why-ai-gross-margins-are-so-much-lower-than-saas-and-what-that-means-for-your-business/). A guide on treating LLM API cost as COGS for wrappers. — [beancount.io](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/13/llm-api-cost-cogs-ai-wrapper-gross-margin-guide)

### Inferences
- **Worked example:** a $19/mo B2C tool with 7% monthly churn has an average lifetime of about 14 months, for an LTV of about $270 before fees. After about 5–6% in MoR fees and any LLM COGS, CAC through paid ads must stay under about $80–100. This is why organic channels (SEO, marketplaces) matter.
- **Margin advantage of non-AI tools:** a solo founder with a non-AI or light-AI tool can keep about 85–95% gross margin after MoR fees. An LLM-heavy tool at flat pricing can fall below 50%.
- **Payment-provider choice:**
  - Paddle is the low-acquisition-risk MoR.
  - Stripe Managed Payments is the likely default for Stripe users once it leaves preview.
  - Polar and Creem are cheaper but newer.
  - Shopify billing is effectively 0% for most solo developers.

### Gaps
- No primary 2025–2026 benchmark specific to solo or indie SaaS ARPU was found. Baremetrics is the closest proxy.
- No B2B SaaS trial-to-paid benchmark from ChartMogul or ProfitWell was retrieved in primary form. Commonly cited figures (about 15–25% opt-in trial, about 2–5% freemium) were not re-verified in this pass.

## 6. Marketplace and ecosystem opportunities: where demand exists and competition is weak

### Takeaway
Marketplaces have verified buyer demand and favorable economics, notably Shopify's 0% share on the first $1M. TrustMRR's Shopify-billed startups show the highest median revenue among mainstream providers. Other weak spots worth targeting:
- **Chrome extensions** that were bought and turned into adware or spyware, which pushes away users who want a trustworthy replacement.
- **WordPress governance turmoil** (the forced ACF-to-SCF fork), which eroded trust in incumbents.

The evidence for specific openings is thinner than for the overall pattern.

### Cited Findings
- **Shopify App Store size and solo outcomes:**
  - The store passed 12,320 apps in January 2025, up 27% YoY. — [Popupsmart Shopify stats](https://community.popupsmart.com/t/shopify-statistics-global-shopify-snapshot-2025/332)
  - Documented solo and two-person apps cluster at $2K–$20K MRR, and niche apps reach $5K–$50K MRR with 100–2,000 paying customers (blog-tier). — [ecomappguide](https://ecomappguide.pages.dev/posts/2026/02/shopify-apps-business-guide-for-builders); [adsx](https://adsx.com/blog/how-much-shopify-app-developers-make)
  - Anecdote: a simple shipping-calculator app earning $8K/mo. — [DEV solo Shopify dev](https://dev.to/jmsdevlab/how-i-built-7-shopify-apps-as-a-solo-developer-in-ireland-2nfp)
  - On TrustMRR, startups paid through Shopify billing have a median all-time revenue of $1,015, versus $279 for Stripe. E-commerce is the #1 category by revenue. — [TrustMRR stats](https://trustmrr.com/stats)
- **Chrome Web Store trust failures:**
  - "Save image as Type" (1M+ users, "Featured" badge) reportedly changed hands around November 2025 and then hijacked affiliate links (secondary repost). — [longform.social](https://longform.social/post/did:plc:xj2drxwuk2r3tfelpnw2uqog/3mhesbbaubpy2)
  - The ShadyPanda campaign (December 2025): extensions built up legitimacy over years, then pushed malicious updates affecting about 4.3M Chrome and Edge users. — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/12/01/chrome_edge_malicious_browser_extensions/)
  - Google purged about 500 harmful extensions that stole data from about 1.7M users (date unspecified, likely older). — [ITPro](https://itpro.com/security/cyber-security/354776/google-purges-500-harmful-chrome-extensions-from-its-web-store); [LinuxSecurity](https://www.linuxsecurity.com/news/vendors-products/500-chrome-extensions-caught-stealing-private-data-of-1-7-million-users)
  - Older precedent: "Add to Feedly" (about 30K users) was sold and turned into adware in 2014. — [PCWorld](https://www.pcworld.com/article/443467/spammers-buy-chrome-extensions-and-turn-them-into-adware.html)
- **WordPress:**
  - In October 2024, WordPress.org used plugin guideline 18 to fork WP Engine's Advanced Custom Fields into "Secure Custom Fields". Auto-updating sites were switched to it automatically.
  - The fork covers only the free features, not ACF PRO.
  - Security consultant Tim Nash disputed that SCF is any more secure.
  - Critics said the move undermines trust in the plugin ecosystem.
  — [WP Tavern](https://wptavern.com/acf-plugin-forked-to-secure-custom-fields-plugin); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/12/in-latest-move-against-wp-engine-wordpress-takes-control-of-acf-plugin); [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2024/10/14/wordpress_forks_wpengine_plugin/); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/automattic-receives-backlash-for-cloning-premium-plugin/533589/)
- **Freemius:** a WordPress-centric monetization platform where 45.7% of SaaS makers are solo founders (secondary). — [discury](https://discury.io/playbook-profitable-boring-businesses-vs-ai-startups-8643)

### Inferences
- **Shopify:** look for apps in niche sub-categories, such as a specific vertical, a regional compliance need, or a format a particular merchant type needs. Two signals point to an opening: (a) incumbents rated about 3.5 stars or lower with recent complaints about price hikes or support, and (b) no "Built for Shopify" competitor. The store converts demand that already exists, which suits a solo founder with no audience.
- **Chrome:** after a sale-and-adware incident, a "clean, open-source or privacy-first replacement for [compromised extension]" is a credible wedge. Users actively search for alternatives, and the incident itself gives the replacement its marketing story. Keep it as a free or freemium wedge into a paid product, since Chrome extension monetization alone is weak.
- **WordPress:** governance uncertainty plus incumbent price hikes create demand for (a) self-hosted, one-time-license alternatives and (b) tools for agencies managing multi-site risk. The price-hike evidence is not verified in this pass. Treat it as a hypothesis.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 data on WordPress plugin price hikes, or on how the WP Engine dispute resolved after October 2024, was found. A follow-up search is needed.
- No quantitative "bad-review incumbent" list for the Shopify App Store was found. That would need direct scraping or review analysis.
- Slack, HubSpot, Notion, Atlassian and Zoom marketplace revenue distributions for solo developers were not found.
- The Chrome extension sale market (prices, frequency) is documented only anecdotally.

## 7. Selection criteria for a solo founder in late 2026, and specific opportunities with evidence

### Takeaway
Choose products where code is the cheap part and the hard parts build up over time: a buyer channel that already exists, workflow or integration lock-in, domain specificity, and non-LLM margins. Avoid anything a foundation-model feature release or a weekend of vibe coding can copy.

### Cited Findings (the evidence behind each criterion)
1. **A distribution channel with buyers already in it (marketplace, search intent, or owned audience), chosen before building.** Downloads are flat (+2–3%) while submissions are up 84%. TrustMRR revenue correlates most with age and Domain Rating. Product Hunt spikes fade within about a week. — [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/113213-apple-app-store-inundated-low-quality-vibecoded-apps.html); [TrustMRR stats](https://trustmrr.com/stats); [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/product-hunt-is-a-hub-of-builders-and-high-karma-hunters-your-end-users-arent-there-6d18cffdae)
2. **B2B, or prosumers who pay as businesses.** NRR is 82% for B2B vs. 49% for B2C, and average B2B churn is about 3.5%. — [Subjolt/ChartMogul](https://www.subjolt.com/guides/churn-rate-benchmarks/); [Vitally/Recurly](https://www.vitally.io/post/saas-churn-benchmarks)
3. **Not on a foundation-model roadmap, and not a thin AI wrapper.** OpenAI has absorbed file analysis, browsing and custom GPTs, and AI monthly plans retain 36% worse. — [DEV](https://dev.to/thesythesis/the-wrapper-kc7); [RevenueCat 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md)
4. **Gross margin of 80% or more after LLM costs.** If AI is used, price by usage or credits. AI products average about 45–53% gross margin vs. 80–90% for SaaS. — [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/have-ai-gross-margins-really-turned-the-corner/); [softwareseni](https://www.softwareseni.com/why-ai-gross-margins-are-so-much-lower-than-saas-and-what-that-means-for-your-business/)
5. **Pricing that filters for real buyers: hard paywall or trial, annual plans, ARPU of about $30/mo or more.** Hard paywalls show 5x the conversion and 8x the revenue per install. Annual plans retain better at low ARPA. — [RevenueCat 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md)
6. **Defensibility beyond code: integrations, proprietary or accumulating data, compliance or domain knowledge, reviews and rank.** Clones are proliferating, and in the Shopify store, ranking depends on reviews and Built-for-Shopify status. — [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/113213-apple-app-store-inundated-low-quality-vibecoded-apps.html); [ecomappguide](https://ecomappguide.pages.dev/posts/2026/02/shopify-apps-business-guide-for-builders)
7. **A weak incumbent whose users are already searching for an exit.** Typical triggers are price hikes, ownership changes, adware, or governance turmoil. — [The Register (ShadyPanda)](https://www.theregister.com/2025/12/01/chrome_edge_malicious_browser_extensions/); [WP Tavern](https://wptavern.com/acf-plugin-forked-to-secure-custom-fields-plugin)
8. **Plausible to sell:** profitable, low-maintenance, and transferable. Small SaaS sells at about 3.9x profit (SDE), and buyers increasingly require earnouts or holdbacks. — [BigIdeasDB](https://bigideasdb.com/state-of-saas-valuations-2026); [Paddle/Acquire](https://paddle.com/news/industry/acquire-market-report-2025-saas-m-and-a)

### Inferences: specific opportunities with relatively strong evidence
- **A. Niche Shopify apps for specific merchant verticals or compliance needs.** Evidence:
  - E-commerce is TrustMRR's #1 revenue category.
  - Shopify-billed startups have the highest median revenue among mainstream providers ($1,015).
  - Shopify takes 0% of the first $1M.
  - The store converts demand that already exists.
  - Documented solo apps reach $2K–$20K MRR.
  Approach: target sub-categories where the top incumbents have weak ratings or recent price hikes and no Built-for-Shopify competitor. Keep any AI optional and metered.
- **B. Trust-first replacements for compromised or sold Chrome extensions, used as a wedge into a paid prosumer or B2B tool.** Evidence: the 2025 ShadyPanda campaign (4.3M users) and the 1M-user "Save image as Type" case show a recurring pattern of displaced users looking for safe alternatives. Risks: Chrome monetization is weak, and the specific sale cases are secondary-sourced.
- **C. "Boring" vertical B2B workflow SaaS with integrations, such as contractor back-office or document workflows.** Evidence:
  - Better B2B retention economics (NRR 82% vs. 49%).
  - Non-AI margins.
  - The SEO/Domain Rating correlation supports content-led acquisition.
  The niche-specific evidence (for example, a lien waiver automator for subcontractors) is blog-tier.
- **D. Self-hosted or one-time-license alternatives in ecosystems with governance or pricing turmoil, such as WordPress.** Evidence: the ACF-to-SCF fork and the documented backlash. The 2025–2026 price-hike evidence is unverified and needs follow-up.

### Gaps
- None of these opportunities has been validated with keyword volumes, review scraping or install counts in this pass. Each needs direct marketplace data before a commitment.
- No primary data was found comparing solo success rates by channel (marketplace vs. SEO vs. audience). The ranking above is an inference from TrustMRR correlations and RevenueCat and Shopify data.
